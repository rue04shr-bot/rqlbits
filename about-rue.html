hi. welcome to whatever this is.

☕✨

scientists say the human brain can store massive amounts of information. unfortunately mine mostly stores:

  • embarrassing moments from 2018
  • fake arguments i won in the shower
  • song lyrics i don't even consciously remember learning
  • and highly unnecessary observations at 2am

this blog exists because indian households don't really believe in concepts like: “privacy” or “peaceful self-reflection.”

keeping a diary? impossible.

“kya likh rahi ho?”
“dikhao zara.”
“shayari kar rahi hai kya?”

and if you sit quietly for too long, family members start doing investigative journalism level tanka jhanki.

so instead of hiding notes apps, unfinished journals, and emotionally concerning thoughts inside random folders, i made this little internet corner.

here you'll mostly find: funny incidents, oversharing, books that changed my personality temporarily, unnecessary opinions, and occasional breakdowns disguised as humour.

some posts will be deep. some will be stupid. most will probably begin with: “so basically…”

anyway. welcome. please remove your shoes before entering my chaos ✨
