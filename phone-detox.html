i tried living without my phone for 72 hours

📵

day one felt illegal. i kept reaching for my phone every six minutes like a lab rat pressing a dopamine button. by hour eight, i was staring at my ceiling contemplating the concept of boredom. terrifying experience honestly.

the first symptom of phone withdrawal is apparently opening and closing your fridge every twenty minutes. the second is suddenly becoming deeply invested in observing birds outside your window like a retired grandfather.

“maybe my attention span wasn't destroyed by technology. maybe it was just hiding under seventeen notifications and an unhealthy attachment to short-form content.”

by the second day, time started moving differently. mornings felt slower. tea tasted more dramatic. i reorganised my bookshelf alphabetically for absolutely no reason and somehow convinced myself this counted as healing.

i also discovered that silence is weirdly loud when you aren't drowning it out with tiktok audio and random spotify playlists called things like “crying in a european city at 2am”.

did i become a completely transformed enlightened human being? absolutely not. but i did stop checking my lock screen every eleven seconds like a Victorian child waiting for tragic news.

honestly? i think my brain just needed a nap from the internet.
